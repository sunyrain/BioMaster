# EQIR V3 与既有工作的可证伪差异 V5（2026-08-14）

状态：候选差异已冻结但未验证。V5 新增 AD-LSF 对非对称门控、动态分解、潜在信号协调和双向交互对齐的直接先例边界；复杂融合架构不再进入 EQIR 核心新颖性。

| 既有方向 | 已有工作 | 我们不主张的新颖性 | EQIR 候选差异 | 必须通过的证伪测试 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| drug_centric_listwise_ranking | BRDTI; NeuRank | Drug-centric or listwise ranking is not new and is only an auxiliary loss. | The candidate estimand is the incremental value of qualified structure evidence after a frozen cross-fitted nonstructural offset, under both query directions. | FULL_EQIR_WITHOUT_DRUG_QUERY_LOSS and FULL_EQIR_WITHOUT_TARGET_QUERY_LOSS; both grouped directional bootstraps and within-drug permutation gate. |
| confidence_aware_multimodal_fusion | DrugCMF | Confidence-aware 1D/3D fusion is not claimed. | Qualification is a deterministic label-independent receptor/QC predicate; the optional branch may only fit cross-fitted residuals and cannot relearn the full label. | Compare full EQIR with no qualification and with no residualization under identical frozen base predictions. |
| structure_confidence_fusion | ConfDTI | Structure-quality weighting or pLDDT-guided fusion is not claimed. | Experimental evidence availability controls whether a residual increment is legally defined; quality does not supply a generic learned fusion weight. | Qualification-independence audit plus target-QC-feature ablation and exact fallback equality test. |
| target_prior_causal_debiasing | TAPB | Generic target-prior or causal debiasing is not claimed. | EQIR does not estimate a causal target-prior intervention; it asks whether target-local structural evidence explains held-out residual interaction signal beyond a frozen base. | Within-target and within-drug score-permutation nulls, macro-directional paired bootstrap, and no-residualization ablation. |
| mutual_molecule_protein_retrieval_and_optimal_transport | KGOT | Mutual retrieval and optimal transport are not claimed; EQIR uses no OT pseudo-labeling. | The two query directions constrain the incremental residual value rather than define a new retrieval architecture. | Remove each query loss separately; require structure increment to retain both directional gains versus the nonnovel blend. |
| cold_start_relational_sequence_alignment | CrossLinker | Cold-start alignment is not claimed as a new mechanism. | Scaffold and homology cold axes are identification and stress-test axes for the qualified residual, not the source of novelty. | Both target-homology-cluster and drug-scaffold bootstrap lower bounds must exceed zero. |
| structure_as_privileged_training_information | LUPI protein binding affinity | Optional structure with sequence fallback is not claimed by itself. | When qualification is zero, the final logit is definitionally and bitwise the frozen base; when one, structure can only explain a cross-fitted pseudo-residual. | Bitwise exact fallback audit and BASE_PLUS_RESIDUAL_WITHOUT_EXACT_FALLBACK_CONSTRAINT ablation. |
| pocket_detection_and_structure_fusion | EMPDTA; PocketDTA; PHGDTI | Pocket detection, pocket encoding, or atom-residue fusion is not claimed. | The method consumes a frozen strict experimental-pocket protocol and studies incremental evidence value; it does not propose a pocket encoder. | Receptor/hash/redocking qualification audit; pocket/QC inputs cannot read current-fold interaction labels. |
| missing_not_at_random_dti_labels | FNML | MNAR interaction-label learning is not claimed. | The missing object is structural evidence observation, not an unobserved DTI label; missing evidence forces an exact base estimand rather than an imputed negative. | Verify all missing or failed structure routes have a(t)=0 and zero logit delta; never encode absence as y=0. |
| nonhomologous_pocket_target_fishing | Pocket Hopping | Nonhomologous pocket hopping is an application, not the algorithm claim. | Any novel-target case is supported only if the same frozen qualified-residual mechanism generalizes under homology-cold evaluation. | Homology-cluster bootstrap, locked S3 confirmation, and separate new-target application audit. |
| arbitrary_missing_modality_fusion | FMIN | Dynamic skipping or arbitrary missing-modality fusion is not claimed. | EQIR uses a frozen nonstructural offset, deterministic eligibility, residual-only supervision, and an exact functional invariant rather than a flexible modality fusion network. | No-qualification, no-residualization, and no-exact-fallback ablations must each be inferior to full EQIR. |
| dynamic_sequence_graph_domain_adversarial_fusion | MMTF-DTI | Dynamic sequence/graph fusion and domain-adversarial alignment are not claimed or used as the core. | The candidate contribution is evidence-qualified incremental residual estimation with directional value constraints, not a new encoder or domain discriminator. | Beat same-data DTIAM, BioMaster V1, and the frozen nonnovel blend with identical cold axes and five-seed evidence. |
| orthogonal_sequential_multimodal_fusion | Orthogonal Sequential Fusion | Orthogonal representations or orthogonal sequential fusion are not claimed; the term is removed from the candidate claim. | The structure branch is constrained by label-independent legal eligibility, a frozen cross-fitted offset, exact no-evidence invariance, and two directional incremental-value gates rather than an orthogonality loss. | Qualification, no-residualization, no-exact-fallback, and both query-loss ablations plus dual-axis grouped bootstrap. |
| generic_residual_multimodal_fusion | RRF-Net; RDFNet | Residual multimodal feature fusion is not claimed; residual networks and residual fusion long predate EQIR. | Residual learning is restricted to an induced probability increment over a frozen strongest nonstructural offset and is zero by construction outside a label-independent eligible set. | Full mechanism must beat direct fusion and no-residualization controls while satisfying bitwise fallback and both directional gates. |
| fine_grained_atom_residue_gated_residual_fusion | GADFDTI; GATv2-TransDTI | Fine-grained atom-residue maps; graph-sequence cross-attention; bidirectional gating; and residual fusion are not claimed. | GADFDTI and GATv2-TransDTI learn complete warm-start interaction predictors; EQIR conditionally permits only a probability-scale increment over a frozen cold-protocol base and must equal that base when evidence is ineligible. | Compare full EQIR with direct structure fusion and no-residualization variants under S3; require both cold-axis bootstrap lower bounds and both directional value gates. |
| baseline_relative_group_risk_safe_adaptation | Batch Spectral Shrinkage; Fair Classifiers that Abstain without Harm; Calibrated Regression Against An Adversary Without Regret | Safe transfer, no-harm learning, baseline regret, CVaR and group-risk constraints are not claimed as new. | The candidate restricts a structure adapter by a label-independent experimental-evidence predicate, freezes the cross-fitted DTI base, measures relative ranking regret separately over target and old-drug query groups, controls both upper tails, and is bitwise identical to the base outside eligibility. | Compare full safe-dual-query EQIR with the ordinary dual-query objective, target-only and drug-only risk constraints, no-qualification and no-exact-fallback variants, all TCP-inspired controls and both TAPB-inspired controls; require five-seed, dual-cold-axis and both directional gates. |
| multisource_decoupling_cross_property_attention_dynamic_multibranch_loss | MFDR-DTI | Multisource feature decoupling, homogeneous feature preservation, shared cross-property attention and dynamic multibranch loss weighting are not claimed as new. | EQIR does not depend on a new fusion backbone: it conditionally adapts a frozen cross-fitted DTI base only where experimental evidence is label-independently eligible, is exactly identical to the base otherwise, and controls base-relative ranking-regret upper tails separately for target and old-drug queries. | Compare full EQIR with frozen-base, capacity-matched direct multiview fusion and multibranch auxiliary-supervision controls under identical leak-free S1-S3 folds and five seeds; require both cold-axis confidence intervals, target-macro noninferiority, drug-macro improvement and both directional permutation gates. |
| asymmetric_dynamic_gating_latent_signal_coordination_bidirectional_alignment | AD-LSF | Asymmetric dynamic gating, anisotropic/dynamic decomposition, latent signal coordination and bidirectional interactive alignment are not claimed as new. | EQIR leaves the fusion backbone deliberately small and instead defines where structure may change a frozen cross-fitted base, requires exact identity outside label-independent eligibility and constrains the upper tail of base-relative ranking regret in both target and old-drug query groups. | Compare full EQIR with corrected capacity-matched asymmetric-gated and bidirectional-alignment controls on the same S1-S3 folds and five seeds; require dual cold-axis confidence intervals, target-macro noninferiority, drug-macro improvement, both query permutation gates and exact fallback. |
